# Solarity Threshold Analysis

The Solarity threshold represents a critical point in the SolarCoin ecosystem where the network incentive (SLR) reduces the Levelized Cost of Energy (LCOE) to a point of parity or superiority against traditional energy sources.

## Core Definition
Solarity is defined as the state where the combined value of solar energy production and SolarCoin rewards creates a positive economic feedback loop that accelerates deployment beyond market-driven rates.

## Theoretical Framework
Based on the Solarity SSRN paper, the threshold is reached when:
- **Cost reduction**: The SLR reward covers the marginal cost of solar installation/financing.
- **Network Parity**: The value of being in the SolarCoin network (Global Solar Asset Registry) exceeds the cost of data submission.

## Key Drivers
1. **SolarCoin Price (P_slr)**: Higher prices pull the threshold closer.
2. **Solar Efficiency (E_sol)**: Technological improvements lower the base LCOE.
3. **Grid Parity (G_p)**: The cost of alternative energy sources.

## Economic Impact
When the threshold is crossed, solar energy becomes the "default" choice for rational economic actors, independent of government subsidies.
